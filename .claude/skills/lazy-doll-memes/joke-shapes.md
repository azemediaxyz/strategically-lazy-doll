# Joke shapes that already went viral

Every meme starts from one of these. Keep the shape and the rhythm word for word: same setup, same twist position, same last beat. Swap only the nouns into your world. If you change more than the nouns, it stops being the proven joke.

Numbers are views on Instagram/TikTok and how many times the creator's usual reach the post got (×). Researched Oct 2026; refresh with your own outlier search when you can (see SKILL.md, Step 1).

`[pain]` = something your audience is sick of. `[fix]` = what you sell, said as a result, never as a product name. `[them]` = the people/things that let them down (clients, leads, contractors, the inbox, the algorithm).
