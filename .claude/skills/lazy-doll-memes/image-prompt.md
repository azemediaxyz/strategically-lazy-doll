# The doll image: master prompt + face check

## What you need
- **2 clear photos of yourself** (face visible, good light, no sunglasses). Only ever your own photos, or photos of someone who said yes in writing.
- **1 style reference** (optional): any "doll version of a woman" image whose look you like. It sets the lighting and polish only, never the face.

Works in ChatGPT (image generation), Gemini (Nano Banana), Artlist (GPT Image), or any image model that takes reference photos. Portrait 3:4, make 2 versions each time.

## The prompt (copy, fill the [brackets])

> Image 1 and image 2 are photographs of the subject: [a woman with long brunette hair — describe yours]. Image 3 is a doll-style reference for the look only. Recreate the style and aesthetic of the reference (image 3), but use the woman in images 1 and 2 as the subject. Keep her recognizable facial features, natural proportions, eyes, eye shape, nose, lips, face shape, skin tone and [hair] exactly as in her photos. Do not change her ethnicity or the shape of her eyes. Use the reference only for styling, lighting and overall vibe, NOT for the face.
>
> Look: a pretty, cute, glamorous doll version of herself. Smooth glowing skin, soft doll-like polish, long lashes, glossy pink lips, voluminous glossy [hair], warm flattering light. Slightly doll-ish, polished, but still a natural, beautiful photograph of her. She must look her absolute best.
>
> Scene: [one scene + outfit, see below]. No text, no logos, no watermarks. Leave clear space in the lower part of the frame for a caption.

No style reference? Delete the "Image 3" sentences and keep the rest.

## Pick the scene from the line
- **Tired line** → tired doll: slumped on a pink couch, laptop glowing, eyeliner a little smudged, iced coffee.
- **Petty / savage line** → calm doll in front of chaos: sipping coffee while the office burns behind her, holding a baseball bat in a pink pantsuit, filing nails next to crime-scene tape.
- **Lazy flex line** → pool float, sunglasses on her head, laptop closed; or on the couch while a robot does the dishes.
- **Holiday line** → the costume, described in words (don't upload costume photos).

Change only the scene and outfit between memes. Same prompt otherwise, so your memes look like a series.

## Too real or too plastic?
- Looks like a normal photo → add "more doll-like polish, a touch of Barbie-movie styling".
- Looks like a plastic toy, or not like you → "less plastic, more natural, keep her real face".

## Face check (before anyone sees it)
Put the face next to your real photo. Reject and regenerate if:
- the eyes got smaller, narrower or changed shape;
- the face shape, nose or skin tone changed;
- she looks plain, tired (when the line isn't a tired line) or older;
- hands, teeth or the prop are broken.

If it's almost right, ask for a fix on that one image: "Make her face match images 1 and 2 exactly. Keep everything else identical." Then compare the fix to the original: sometimes the first one is better.

## Things models block
Sheer outfits, corsets/stockings, real weapons pointed at people, real crime-scene photos, celebrity names, brand names (don't write "Barbie"). Rephrase in plain words and try again.
