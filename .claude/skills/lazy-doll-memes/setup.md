# First-run setup interview

Ask these one at a time, conversationally. Give an example with each so the user isn't staring at a blank. Then write `lazy-doll-profile.yml` (shape: `profile-template.yml`) and show it back in plain words for a yes.

1. **What do you do, in one line, and who for?** (e.g. "I do bookkeeping for salon owners.") → `business`, `audience.who`
2. **What does your customer complain about to her friends?** Not about your service: about her life. (husbands, kids, being tired, 400 unread emails, girl math) → `audience.what_they_joke_about`
3. **What's the weight she carries that nobody sees?** (e.g. "she's the whole business and nobody notices until something breaks") → `audience.emotional_core`
4. **What drives her crazy at work?** Give me 5: clients, tools, habits, the industry. (late payers, "quick calls", scope creep, the CRM nobody updates) → `targets`
5. **What do you sell, as a result she gets?** (not "monthly bookkeeping" but "never opening a shoebox of receipts again") → `offers`
6. **How mean can we be?** savage (default, gets forwarded) / cheeky / soft. Anything off-limits? → `voice`
7. **Send me 2 photos of you**, face clear, good light. And describe your hair. Optional: a doll-look image you love as a style reference. → `look`

After saving: write 3 sample lines from `joke-shapes.md` using the new profile so the user sees it working, then go to SKILL.md Step 3.

Re-run setup anytime with "redo my doll profile".
