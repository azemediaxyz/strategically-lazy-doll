# Before → after: how a viral line becomes a business meme

The rule: keep the shape, swap the nouns. These show it working, and what gets cut.

| Viral original | Swapped (AI business owner) | Kept? Why |
|---|---|---|
| "It's crazy that my 9 month old dog knows what 'no' means but not 7 grown men" | "It's crazy that my AI knows what 'follow up' means but not 7 grown men." | Cut: funny, but about men, not her pain |
| "Life is hard. Make it harder. Start a brand." | "Business is hard. Make it easier. Hire AI." | Kept: same rhythm, flipped ending |
| "I might look like a normal girl, but I argue with suppliers overseas at 2am" | "I might look like a normal girl, but my AI is closing leads at 2am." → "…but I argue with AI at 2am." | Kept the 2nd: her doing it is funnier than the AI doing it |
| "I'm tired of making content. Can you all just place your orders please" | "I'm tired of making content. Can you all just run my free AI visibility check and pay me please." | Kept: the swap points at a real free offer |
| "Why do people say chicken as a term for coward? Have you ever met a chicken?" | "Why do people say they can't trust AI? Have you met people?" | Kept: shortest version wins |

## Same shapes, other businesses

**Bookkeeper for salons**
- "It's crazy that my calculator knows what 'due date' means but not 14 grown clients."
- "Not to be dramatic, but chasing the invoices I sent, reconciling the receipts I begged for and running the business I chose is making it increasingly difficult to be a delight to be around."

**Wedding photographer**
- "When a bride asks for 'just a few quick edits' and your face has subtitles."
- "Currently accepting applications for someone to answer 'what's your availability' every day. Humans need not apply."

**Fitness coach**
- "Believe in yourself the way your client believes the 'cheat day' was just one day."
- "What am I for Halloween? A coach whose clients actually did the homework. Terrifying, I know."
